What we handle

Fractional project accounting for the full billing cycle. Use all of it, or just the part you need.

Schedule of Values
We build a clear, defensible Schedule of Values at the start of the job so every later pay application lines up with it.
Monthly AIA pay applications
Accurate progress billing, prepared on time and in the format your general contractor expects.
Compliance paperwork
The supporting documents that go with each application, tracked and submitted so nothing stalls a payment.
Cashflow follow-through
We watch what has been billed and what has been paid, so late payments get noticed early.
Retainage collection
We stay with the job through closeout and work to collect the final retainage payment.

How it works

  1. Intro call

    Tell us about your current jobs and how billing works for you today.

  2. Set up the job

    We review the contract and build the Schedule of Values.

  3. Bill every month

    You send progress. We prepare and submit the application and paperwork.

  4. Close out

    We follow through to the final retainage payment.
